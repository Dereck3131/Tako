# App Screenshots

Drop your real Tako mobile app screenshots (9:19.5 portrait ratio) in this folder:

- `splits-home-light.png` & `splits-home-dark.png`
- `new-split-light.png` & `new-split-dark.png`
- `scan-to-join-light.png` & `scan-to-join-dark.png`
- `activity-light.png` & `activity-dark.png`
- `profile-light.png` & `profile-dark.png`
